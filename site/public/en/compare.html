iphone-use vs other iPhone automation tools

Several projects let an agent or a test drive an iPhone. They differ in what they target, how they reach a real phone, and what they are for. This page compares facts, not rankings.

Checked 2026-10-27, from each project's own README. Projects change; follow the links for the current state.

What iphone-use focuses on

  • Your own real iPhone and the apps already on it, signed in: Health, banking, chat apps, your app under test. A daemon on your Mac hosts the phone.
  • Its own XCTest device runner, which replaced WebDriverAgent in v0.14.0 and keeps the WebDriverAgent HTTP API. Requests to it are signed per launch.
  • Explicit action outcomes: applied, not sent or unknown, with retry_safe; taps on covered elements are refused.
  • Deterministic flow replay from a reviewed flow registry, with no model in the loop.
  • People and agents on one phone: a browser page and an iOS app for remote control, one owner at a time.
  • The host is a Mac, and it drives iPhones (and iOS Simulators in preview), not Android devices.

Which one fits

📱 Everyday apps on your own iPhone

Reading data out of apps with no API, with you able to watch and take over: what iphone-use is built for.

🤝 iOS and Android from one tool

agent-device and Mobile MCP both cover iOS and Android, simulators and physical devices, and connect to device clouds.

🧪 A maintained WebDriver test suite

Appium with the XCUITest driver is the established WebDriver ecosystem for cross-platform end-to-end tests.

🐍 A Python agent with its own loop

ios-agent ships a terminal agent, an MCP server and a Python library, for the iOS Simulator and real iPhones.

A measured comparison

ℹ️ Maintainer measurement, not an independent benchmark
The iphone-use README reports, for the same iPhone 13 over USB (October 2026): reading the screen took 0.08–0.135 s with the iphone-use runner and about 0.7 s with agent-device; a tap plus waiting for the screen to settle took 1.0 s and 2.8–3.3 s. No other tool on this page was measured by the project. Speed claims need the same device, actions and settling conditions to compare.
